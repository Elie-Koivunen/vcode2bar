3. Specification
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The bank barcode follows the public specification "Pankkiviivakoodi-opas" (bank barcode guide,
version 5.3) published by Finance Finland (Finanssiala ry). vcode2bar implements that public
specification; it does not include the document.
